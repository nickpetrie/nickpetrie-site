## Identity usage
NP: use the name for introductions, the compact NP for avatars. One stem-width of clear space outside the bare mark. Avatar proof uses 64% of width for the artwork; preserve this through square/circle variants. No extra slash, margin graphic, underline, ligature or staggered baseline. Do not stretch, add shadow, bevel or outline.
WNMP: full title leads; by Nick Petrie endorses. Use the paired-rule motif near the masthead or sign-off, not every heading. Keep at least one motif-height of clear space outside a standalone motif. For unfamiliar readers, pair the motif with the title. Do not insert NP into the WNMP title.
Small-size starting points: NP avatar >=32 px; full WNMP masthead >=240 px wide. Below that use a compact title or motif with an accessible publication name. These are review starting points, not claims of completed exhaustive optical testing.

## Shared typography
Source Sans 3: Regular for supporting text, Semibold for Nick's name and labels, Bold for the WNMP masthead. Source Serif 4 Display Regular for editorial headlines and quotations; the text cut for long reading if wanted. Suggested reading text 18–20 px with 1.5–1.65 leading; email fallback Arial/Georgia. Let Claude adapt responsive layout while preserving hierarchy and legibility.
Labels 12–14 px, modest tracking, no long all-caps sentences. Source notes 13–14 px. Pull quotes 28–40 px, 1.15–1.25 leading. Body links visibly underlined; colour alone must not identify a link.

## WNMP editorial language
Suggested promise: Clear thinking about the products, teams and decisions shaping news.
Personality: authored, practical, constructively challenging and conversational. It has a point of view without assuming the reader agrees.
1. Signature: paired rules + full title + byline; yellow field for mastheads and issue promotion.
2. Hierarchy: topic label -> serif headline -> concise standfirst -> author/date. Reading time or issue number only if supported by real content; never fabricate them.
3. Section break: one thin ink rule with a sans label. Labels such as In practice or A useful example are optional, not a publishing commitment.
4. Pull quote: large serif, ink on paper, single 3 px yellow left rule; attribution below. One memorable idea, not a second headline.
5. Annotation: short practical implication, question or caveat; sans text, yellow edge. Do not turn every paragraph into a card.
6. Reference: source title plus one useful sentence explaining why it matters; source/author/date below. No decorative icons.
7. Reader prompt: one direct question and a reply invitation. Keep the tone human.
8. Diagrams: direct labels, ink strokes, simple boxes/arrows; yellow highlights one decision or insight. Avoid colour-only meaning. Label sources and distinguish hypothetical diagrams from evidence.
9. Sign-off: Nick, optional paired rules, one reply or subscription action. Avoid repeated branding inside an issue.
These elements are independent building blocks; do not require every issue to use every block.

## Colour and imagery
Personal identity: predominantly warm white with ink, small yellow accents. Strong yellow fields primarily signal WNMP.
WNMP: confident yellow entry points; warm-white reading areas. Use only one strong emphasis treatment in a local composition. No gradients, 3D finishes, decorative patterns or extra accent colours without review.
Ink on warm white 13.75:1; ink on yellow 8.85:1; muted on warm white 5.56:1. Yellow on paper 1.55:1 is decorative only. Muted on yellow 3.58:1 is unsuitable for normal text: use ink. Decorative rule is not a sufficient input boundary. Provide visible keyboard focus.
Real portrait photography for Nick: natural light, calm setting, comfortable expression, generous crop. Keep colour natural; avoid heavy duotone. WNMP: purposeful screenshots, clear diagrams and occasional documentary photography. No generic AI newsroom imagery or device mockup decoration. Captions, credit and provenance remain visible.

## Promotional applications
Personal: name + one thought, ample paper and ink. WNMP: title + issue headline + author, with a confident yellow field. Suggested masters: 1200x630 landscape, 1080x1350 portrait, 1080 square; these are working canvases, not current platform upload guarantees. Keep type clear of edges and test crops. Avoid invented testimonials, issue dates or statistics.
