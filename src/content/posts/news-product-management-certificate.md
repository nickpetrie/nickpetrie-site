As I said on Twitter yesterday I am really proud to see the NPA launch this course.

It's been really thoughtfully designed to be just whats needed for those of you interested specifically in the challenges of product in a Newsroom/publishing environment.

For those of you wondering what the course is and what it's designed to do:

*The NPMC is a rigorous, ****part-time****, and ****virtual**** program ****designed for mid to senior-level news professionals**** that brings together a diverse cohort of 25-30 participants from across the global news industry.*

It's going to create amazing problem solvers, a fantastic global networks of news product practitioners and help individuals make career transitions that are good for them and their organisations.
