I finally got the chance to listen to the first episode of [Better Leaders](https://better-leaders.simplecast.com/), hosted by Anita who has just founded the [Better Leaders Lab](https://betterleaderslab.com/)

It's a really interesting exploration of Trei Brundett's evolution as a leader at Vox, ultimately taking on the first COO role at Vox in 2017.

One of my favourite parts was early on, when they talked about learning to be a good leader from the people who work for you. Rather than modelling exclusively from good or bad bosses.

I also loved how much Trei talked about the power of genuine collaboration - teams bringing expertise together to become more than the sum of their parts. Something that's really hard to do, but magic when it happens.  

I can't wait to see who is on it next.
